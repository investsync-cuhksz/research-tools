Example of a strong page 1 (CATL, 8 Nov 2026, from a published-quality initiation; ANOTHER company: copy its depth,
argument and structure, never its facts or figures).

Headline: Unit profits are resetting, but at ~10x ex-cash earnings the reset is priced in. Initiating at BUY, fair
value CNY 335 (50% DCF, 50% P/E). Our FY27E EPS is 18% below consensus; the stock still screens cheap.

Investment thesis
- The global scale leader is still taking share. CATL installed 39.1% of the world's EV batteries in Jan-Sep 2026
  (BYD 15.2%), its share outside China rose to 33.7%, and it is #1 in storage. Q3 2026 revenue hit a record CNY
  165.2bn (+58.6% YoY) on more than 260 GWh of shipments.
- Profit per kWh is resetting lower, and we model it below the Street. Q3 gross margin fell to 20.7% (Q3 2025:
  25.8%) on low-value storage projects, a new 2% battery consumption tax and a cut export VAT rebate. Assuming CATL
  passes half of the ~CNY 27bn gross FY27E policy cost to customers, we forecast FY27E EPS CNY 19.12, 18% below
  consensus.
- Even on lower numbers the stock is cheap: 13.3x our FY27E EPS, or 10.5x excluding CNY 53/share of net cash.
- Cash returns cushion the wait: a 50% payout plus a buyback return roughly 4.5-6% of market value over a year.

Key risks: tax pass-through fails (0% pass-through cuts FY27E profit by ~CNY 10bn: the bear case, CNY 203); storage
price war and mix dilution; working capital normalises.

What to watch: Q4 gross margin (we assume 21.0%) at FY results ~Mar 2027; customer price agreements after the rebate
removal on 1 Jan 2027; monthly share data; buyback pace.

Why it works: one debate in the headline and a side taken; where the house differs from consensus it says by how much
and why; each risk is quantified and tied to the bear case; what to watch names the number to check.
