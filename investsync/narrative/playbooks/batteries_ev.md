- Volume (GWh shipped) and profit per unit of energy explain results better than revenue, because raw-material prices pass through to selling prices.
- Lithium and other raw-material prices move revenue and margins with a lag; check the latest price trend before calling margins.
- Separate power batteries (cars) from energy storage: growth, pricing and customers differ.
- Overseas plants, export tax rules, tariffs and subsidies change unit economics; check for recent policy changes.
- Heavy capex and working-capital swings mean free cash flow can differ sharply from profit in any period.
- Peers are other cell makers (domestic and Korean / Japanese), not upstream material suppliers.
