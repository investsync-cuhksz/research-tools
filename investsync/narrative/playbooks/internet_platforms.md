sectors: internet content, internet retail, interactive media, software, information, entertainment, gaming, electronic gaming, 互联网, 软件
- Revenue by segment matters more than the total: games, advertising, fintech / payments, cloud and e-commerce grow and earn margins very differently.
- User engagement (monthly active users, time spent) leads revenue; monetisation per user explains the gap.
- Adjusted operating profit excludes share-based pay and investment gains; say which basis you use.
- Large listed and unlisted investment stakes can be a big part of value: a sum-of-the-parts view values them separately from the operating business.
- AI and data-centre spending raises capex and depreciation before it adds revenue; judge it by what management says it buys and when returns are expected.
- Regulation (gaming approvals, platform rules, data) and competition for user time are the usual risks.
