- Do not use EV/EBITDA, gross margin or free cash flow: they are not meaningful for banks and insurers.
- Banks: net interest margin, loan growth, credit costs and non-performing loans, capital ratio (CET1) and return on tangible equity drive value; price-to-book against return on equity is the core valuation link.
- Dividends and buybacks depend on capital headroom; the dividend discount model suits mature banks.
- Interest-rate direction moves margins; say which way the company says it is exposed.
- Peers should share the funding model and geography (global banks versus domestic lenders).
