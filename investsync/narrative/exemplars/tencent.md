Example of a strong page 1 (Tencent Holdings, 8 Nov 2026, from a published-quality initiation; ANOTHER company: copy
its depth, argument and structure, never its facts or figures).

Headline: Core engine still compounding; the market prices the AI capex bill as if it never pays back. Initiate at
BUY, fair value HKD 550.

Investment thesis
- Core franchises still compounding. 1H26 revenue +10.1% YoY to RMB 401.2bn (marketing services +20.9%, domestic
  games +11.3%). Operating profit excluding new AI products +18% to RMB 170.5bn, a 42.5% margin. Weixin (1,439m MAU)
  monetises through ads and Mini Shops regardless of who wins the chatbot race.
- The AI bill is large, and we model it in full. 1H26 capex RMB 84.7bn (1H25: 46.6bn); 2Q26 free cash flow RMB
  -13.8bn after compute prepayments; new AI products cost ~RMB 19bn of operating profit in 1H26. We assume capex of
  RMB 195bn (2026E) and 210bn (2027E), easing to ~14% of revenue by 2030E.
- The price already discounts a lot of pain. 11.6x our 2027E non-IFRS EPS vs a FY21-25 average trailing P/E of
  21.3x. Our reverse DCF implies a 12.8% WACC, or core margins ~8pp below our base case for five years.
- Portfolio and balance sheet support. Stakes worth ~HKD 112/share gross (~HKD 79 after a 30% haircut); still net
  cash.

Valuation: HKD 550 = 50% DCF (HKD 595) + 30% P/E (14x 2027E) + 20% sum-of-the-parts. Upside 31% exceeds the +15% Buy
hurdle. Bear / bull DCF: HKD 337 / 888.

Key risks: AI capex keeps rising in 2027-28 without matching revenue. ByteDance and Alibaba lead consumer AI, model
APIs and cloud; ad share risk. Game regulation / hit cycle; geopolitics.

What to watch: 3Q26 results, 18 Nov: consensus revenue RMB 210.4bn, non-IFRS EPS RMB 7.22 (-4.7% YoY); capex and
2027 framing.

Why it works: one debate stated in the headline and a side taken; each pillar is a claim plus the two or three
figures that prove it; the cost the market fears is quantified and modelled, not waved away; what the price implies
is stated as a number; risks are specific and tied to the bear case; the next dated event says what to look for.
